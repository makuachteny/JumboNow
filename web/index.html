⚡ Features

Everything Jumbos need,
nothing they don't.

From dining menus to live shuttle maps, JumboNow replaces a dozen apps with one beautiful experience.

🎭

Events & RSVP

Browse events by 8 categories, RSVP instantly, get QR-code tickets, invite friends, and rate events after attending.

🍽️

Dining Menus

Real-time menus for all 11 dining locations with nutrition, allergens, diet icons, and open/closed status.

🚌

Live Shuttle Tracking

Track 7 Tufts shuttle routes in real-time with live bus GPS positions, ETAs, and full schedules.

🍕

Free Food Alerts

Get notified instantly when free food appears on campus. Filter by 8 food types. Post alerts with expiration timers.

👥

Friends & Messaging

Add friends, create groups, send real-time messages. See mutual friends, suggested connections, and friend activity.

🗺️

Campus Map

Interactive map with 19+ buildings, search, 6 category filters, and walking/driving/transit directions.

🎫

QR Code Tickets

Digital tickets with scannable QR codes, 8-character confirmation codes, copy/share, and door check-in.

🏆

Achievements & Vibe

Earn badges for attending events. Build your Vibe Score. Showcase campus involvement on your profile.

🔍

Discover & Search

Full-text event search, trending topics, advanced filters by category, price, and date. Debounced results.

🔔

Real-Time Notifications

Instant alerts for event joins, invitations, messages, free food, and more. Filter by type, mark all read.

➕

Create Events

Full event editor with date pickers, 8 gradient presets, 24+ icons, logo upload, poster upload, and ticket config.

🔗

Campus Resources

Quick links to Canvas, SIS, Health Portal, Tufts Reserve, Laundry View, Athletics, and 15+ essential tools.
